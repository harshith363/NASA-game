### About the team or longer description

We’re developing Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer, a 3D browser game that puts students in charge of a five-person lunar crew. Players fly to the Moon, build and staff an outpost, explore by rover, and prepare to return to Earth. Their choices affect what happens next. Greenhouse lights help grow food but draw power, and a solar particle storm can force the crew to pause outdoor work and head for shelter. An archived NASA solar particle observation inspired the storm exercise. The game labels its simulated readings and includes reference data from NASA and NOAA.
